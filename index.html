Coach preferences

Coach combines your saved goalZ progress with private, on-device guidance for everyday questions. It does not pretend to know live news, prices, laws, medical facts or university rules — check official current sources for those.

Try asking:
“Help me choose between two options.”
“Explain a good way to prepare for an interview.”
“Which cyber skill should I learn first?”
“How can I handle a stressful week?”
